01 / Product & interface study

A little record
of the water.

The fish you caught. The pier you stood on. A quiet, personal journal for New York’s saltwater edges.

Private by design. Yours to keep.
  1. 01

    Photo first. Details after.

    Log catch opens the camera, then photo review and details. Choose from Photos or skip anytime. Photos are now core to the first milestone.

  2. 02

    A different pier. Just a swipe away.

    Spots opens where you left off. Swipe sideways between piers; scroll down for notes and catches. All spots brings back your list and map.

  3. —

    No feed. No account. No cloud sync.

    Future app: on-device SwiftUI for iOS 26+. This HTML study tests the flow before native code.
