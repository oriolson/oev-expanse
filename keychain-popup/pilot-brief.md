# Little Proof: one venue pilot

**DRAFT proposal. Price and host terms are not confirmed.**
Public template: keep contact details, actual costs, availability, and event records in a private copy outside this repository.

## The idea

A small keychain table alongside an existing photo booth. Guests choose a printed photo, decorate a clear keychain with stickers, and assemble it themselves. Assembly help is available when needed.

**Photo booth rental and printing are separate.** Materials are purchased and the product is tested; no new product research is needed.

## Proposed pilot

- **Host:** [VENUE] in [AREA], ideally with an existing photo booth.
- **Format:** guests pay Little Proof directly; no host-paid package proposed.
- **Duration:** two hours of guest activity, plus agreed setup/cleanup time.
- **Capacity:** provisionally up to 30 keychains, one per purchase. This leaves a supply buffer based on the owner's stock input; it is not proven throughput. Guest completion time and table seating remain unknown.
- **Availability:** unknown; agree [DATE / TIME] privately.
- **Draft price:** $10 per DIY keychain with stickers and brief assembly help. No separate assembly upsell. Optional charms only if their selection and add-on price are agreed before the event; exclude charm revenue from the estimate.
- **Venue terms:** propose no fixed table fee for this first trial; fee or revenue share is unknown and must be agreed before booking.

## Who brings what

**Little Proof:** keychain materials, stickers, approved optional charms, tested tools, finished samples, simple instructions, price sign, payment setup, stock tally, and table cleanup.

**Venue:** an agreed table and seats, suitable lighting, clear guest access, setup/cleanup access, and a point of contact. Confirm who supplies the furniture rather than assuming it.

**Booth/printing:** venue confirms a working booth or separately arranged printing, compatible physical prints, and any separate guest charge. Little Proof does not rent a booth or print photos.

```text
            + [PAYMENT FEES] + [VENUE FEE / SHARE]

After labor = remainder - ([TOTAL WORK HOURS] x [OWNER HOURLY RATE])
```

At 15 sales, gross revenue is $150; at the proposed 30 cap, it is $300. Neither is a forecast or profit claim. Include already-purchased materials as a cost of use, without charging their original purchase twice. Set aside applicable sales tax; it is not revenue. Confirm whether the displayed price includes it.

Actual costs, payment fees, labor, and venue terms stay unknown in this public template. Agree what revenue a percentage share applies to, including treatment of tax and refunds. Do not accept host terms until the private calculation shows an acceptable return at a conservative sales count.

## Confirm with the host

- Is this a fit for the venue, and what date/time and guest turnout are realistic?
- Is the booth operating then? What do prints cost, and are they compatible?
- Where can the table go, how many seats fit, and when can setup begin?
- Any table fee, minimum guarantee, or revenue share? Who collects and settles it?
- Can Little Proof take payments directly? Any vendor paperwork, tax or insurance requirements, age rules, or promotion expectations?
- What happens if the event is cancelled or the booth/printing stops? Agree refund/stop-service terms; do not promise replacement printing.
