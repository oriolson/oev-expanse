# Little Proof: event checklist

**Reusable draft.** Copy outside the public repository before filling it in. Keep completed checklists, payments, contacts, costs, and dates private.

## Confirm before the event

- [ ] Host, date, two-hour service window, setup/cleanup access, and contact agreed.
- [ ] Table, seats, lighting, access, and booth/printing confirmed; photo fit checked against the tested format.
- [ ] Guest cap, DIY price, any optional charm price, and tax treatment agreed.
- [ ] Venue fees/share, payment responsibility, and settlement timing written down.
- [ ] Required vendor paperwork and cancellation/booth-failure terms confirmed.
- [ ] Private cost check completed; stock counted and repair spares reserved.
- [ ] Guest completion time remains unknown unless measured. Plan to pause new starts if seats or support are full; do not promise a wait time.

## Pack

- [ ] Counted keychain kits and separately marked spares.
- [ ] Stickers; optional charms only if offered at an agreed price.
- [ ] Tested trimming/assembly tools, sorting trays, and finished samples.
- [ ] Simple instructions and approved price sign: booth/printing separate.
- [ ] Charged payment device, charger/power bank, and existing payment method.
- [ ] Private stock/sales tally, pen, cleaning cloth, and scrap bag.

## Set up

- [ ] Arrange photo check/payment, decorating seats, and finished-item check.
- [ ] Keep small parts contained and sharp tools controlled; follow venue age/supervision rules.
- [ ] Display the price and separate printing charge clearly.
- [ ] Check payment connectivity and receipt/refund access without making an unauthorized charge.
- [ ] Agree a fallback payment method; otherwise pause sales if payment is unavailable.
- [ ] Record opening stock privately.

## Customer flow

1. Check the guest has a compatible print and a seat/kit is available.
2. Explain the DIY price and any optional add-on; take payment and offer a receipt.
3. Guest trims/selects the photo, decorates, and assembles. Help only as needed.
4. Check the closure together; guest takes the keychain and remaining photo.
5. Tally the sale, stock used, and any remake. Keep guest photos with the guest.

## During service

- [ ] Track counts only: sold, gross sales, refunds, charms, damaged/remade, and stock left. No guest names or photo files needed.
- [ ] Track approximate completion time and full staff assemblies separately.
- [ ] Reconcile: opening stock = completed keychains out + unusable parts/kits + remaining stock. Note other uses explicitly.
- [ ] Watch the queue and remaining time; stop new starts early enough to finish.
- [ ] If booth/printing fails, pause new sales and follow agreed terms. Do not improvise a printing service.

## Close + five-minute review

- [ ] Finish guests, count remaining stock, pack tools, collect scraps, wipe the table.
- [ ] Confirm venue handback and any fee/share settlement.
- [ ] Record net receipts, materials used, fees, travel, and total work time privately.
- [ ] Answer: How many sold? What remained after costs and labor? Where did guests need help?
- [ ] Ask the host: Would you try this again, and what should change?
- [ ] Write one change for next time; resolve any outstanding refund or product issue.

## Landing-page photos: pending, not an outreach blocker

- [ ] Owner supplies approved sample photos through an agreed private workspace; do not put originals in the public repository.
- [ ] Confirm publication permission for identifiable people and personal photos, including photos visible inside keychains.
- [ ] Once approved: resize/optimize derivatives for the existing image slot, strip location/device metadata, and verify it is absent.
- [ ] Replace the concept illustration and caption without redesigning; add image-specific alt text, update preview notes, and check mobile layout.
- [ ] Get separate approval before any upload, push, or deployment. Approval of an image is not approval to publish the site.
