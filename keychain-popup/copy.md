# Little Proof / No. 03

**One-sheet zine preview for issue #11. Not approved for launch.**

## Direction

Selected name: Little Proof. Keep the current zine design and hands-on voice,
not the original sentimental Little Proof direction.

Less website, more photocopied handout. A large black masthead, one monochrome
halftone keychain illustration, a serif headline, and a plain blue inquiry link.
No navigation, cards, promotional strip, repeated slogans, or separate process
and option sections.

Keeps the restraint of `personal-site/AGENTS.md` and `tide-now/AGENTS.md`:
plain HTML, hard edges, blue links, small labels, no shadows or gradients.
The zine composition is specific to this project, not a shared design system.

## Voice

The friend with scissors meets a DIY show flyer: a direct invitation to join in,
with the occasional photo-booth observation. Specific, casual, not forced slang.
Lead with decorating and assembling your own keychain. Assembly service stays
available as a secondary note, not an equally prominent pitch.
