Copilot custom models

Making the decisions around a custom model understandable—not just the act of starting training.

~ The work

I led design end to end for Copilot custom models, working with a PM and engineers and incorporating internal design feedback. The difficult questions extended beyond configuration: which data to train on, which levers were available, and how teams might monitor and adjust a model after developers began using it.

I approached this as a connected journey. Training guidance mattered as much as the controls themselves.

~ Two decisions, not one

One important distinction was between permission to collect data at the organization level and the choice to include that data in an individual model. I wanted the language and controls to keep those scopes separate.

~ Delivered beta refinements

In June 2024, I delivered UI polish for the limited beta. My design work considered how to communicate training expectations without a reliable duration estimate, and how language-selection tokens could stay consistent with Primer. The aim was to explain what the system knew without promising what it could not.

~ Explored directions

I also explored repository guidance, templates, and guided onboarding to help teams decide where to begin. Those directions addressed the need for training guidance; they are not presented here as shipped features. Monitoring and adjustment remained part of the broader design intent.

~ In perspective

The preview was later retired before general availability. The beta refinements and the later explorations represent two different parts of the work: making the current experience clearer and investigating where it could go next.
